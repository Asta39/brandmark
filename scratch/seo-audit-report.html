00Executive summary

The site itself — copy, product/service depth, structure — is in good shape: every service and product page carries unique, substantial content with FAQs and use-cases, which is exactly what both Google and AI answer engines reward. The gap is almost entirely in the infrastructure around that content: nothing tells search engines what to crawl, nothing tells them what the business is in machine-readable form, and almost no page pairs its topic with "Nairobi" or "Kenya" — the two words local customers and AI assistants actually search with.

Top 5 critical issues
  1. No robots.txt or sitemap.xml — 404 on both
  2. No metadataBase, Open Graph, or Twitter Card tags anywhere
  3. Zero JSON-LD structured data on any of 29 pages
  4. /shop and /cart are client components with no page-specific title/description
  5. No location keyword in any title tag, meta description or H1 except one homepage line
Top 5 quick wins
  1. Add app/sitemap.ts + app/robots.ts — ~30 min, unlocks indexing
  2. Rewrite the 20 dynamic titles to include "Nairobi" — one data.ts pass
  3. FAQPage schema — content already exists on every service & product page
  4. LocalBusiness/Organization JSON-LD on the homepage
  5. Add a real street address / area — currently only "Nairobi, Kenya"

01Technical SEO

Score 45/100
Fully static-generated (SSG) — fast, crawlable HTML Self-hosted fonts, no render-blocking web-font requests Clean, descriptive URL slugs Mobile-responsive, tested at 375px–1280px
Critical

No robots.txt

/robots.txt returns 404. Without it, crawlers have no explicit crawl policy, and — more importantly — no sitemap discovery hint.

FixAdd src/app/robots.ts returning a MetadataRoute.Robots object: allow all, disallow /cart, point sitemap at the production sitemap URL.
Critical

No sitemap.xml

/sitemap.xml returns 404. With 12 product and 8 service detail pages generated via generateStaticParams, there's no single file telling Google (or Bing/IndexNow) all of them exist.

FixAdd src/app/sitemap.ts. Map over products and services from data.ts to emit /products/[slug] and /services/[slug] entries alongside the static routes. Exclude /cart.
High

No metadataBase set

Root layout metadata has no metadataBase. Once deployed, any relative Open Graph image or canonical URL Next.js generates will resolve incorrectly (or not at all), and social share previews will break.

FixSet metadataBase: new URL("https://brandmarkprintmedia.com") in the root layout's metadata export as soon as the domain is live.
High

/shop and /cart carry zero page-specific metadata

Both are "use client" components, and Next.js only reads metadata/generateMetadata from Server Components — so every visitor and crawler sees the generic homepage title ("Brandmark Print Media | Design. Print. Brand.") on the shop page, the single highest-intent page on the whole site.

FixSplit each into a thin server page.tsx that exports metadata and renders a client child (ShopGrid, CartView) for the interactive parts. Give /cart a robots: {"{"}index: false{"}"} instead — it's a transactional, per-visitor page with nothing worth ranking.
Medium

No canonical tags

Follows directly from the missing metadataBase — without it, Next can't auto-generate canonical URLs. Not urgent pre-launch, resolves itself once the fix above lands.

FixVerify canonicals appear in page source once metadataBase is set; add explicit alternates.canonical only where a page has query-string variants (none currently).
Low

lang="en" instead of a Kenya-specific locale

Minor — lang="en-KE" is a small additional signal for regional relevance, though English content targeting Kenya ranks fine under plain en today.

FixOptional: change <html lang="en"> to lang="en-KE" in the root layout.

02Content quality & E-E-A-T

Score 72/100
Every service page: extended description + who-it's-for + included + pricing + process + FAQ Every product page: specs + use-cases + FAQ + related products Founded-2023 date, named client list, real WhatsApp contact — genuine expertise signals
Medium

Testimonials are unattributed and unverifiable

The six client quotes use realistic first-name-plus-initial signatures ("Wanjiru K.", "Otieno M.") without a photo, company link, or verification path. This isn't a technical SEO flag, but it is an E-E-A-T weak point — and structured Review/AggregateRating schema should not be added on top of these, since marking up testimonials that aren't independently verifiable as schema.org Reviews risks a manual action.

FixOnce real orders start, replace with actual client names + company (with permission) or a Google Business Profile review widget you can legitimately mark up.
Medium

No physical street address or neighbourhood

Every location reference on the site stops at "Nairobi, Kenya." For local-pack ranking and Google Business Profile matching, a consistent street address / area (e.g. a specific road or estate) matters — both for NAP (Name-Address-Phone) consistency and for LocalBusiness schema, which requires a real streetAddress to be useful.

FixAdd the actual office/workshop address to the footer and contact section once confirmed for public listing.
Low

/shop category page has thin standalone copy

The shop index itself (before filtering) has only a one-sentence description above the grid. Not a real problem since each of the 12 linked product pages is substantial, but the shop page's own unique text is minimal.

FixLow priority — optional 2–3 sentence intro paragraph covering the shop's breadth (DTF, UV, laser-cut categories) for a bit more on-page depth.
Info

No blog / resource hub

There's no content layer for the kind of question-shaped queries AI Overviews and ChatGPT surface most often — "how much does vehicle branding cost in Nairobi," "DTF vs screen printing," etc. Not a defect for a commerce-first site, but the single highest-leverage addition for both organic reach and AI citation over time.

FixSee Action Plan, Phase 3 — a handful of question-titled articles targeting the keyword map below.

03On-page SEO

Score 55/100
One H1 per page, correctly used everywhere Unique title + description on all 20 dynamic product/service pages Breadcrumb navigation (visual) on every detail page
High

Titles and meta descriptions never include "Nairobi" or "Kenya"

All 20 dynamic pages follow generateMetadata() → title: "{"{"}name{"}"} | Brandmark Print Media", pulled straight from product.description / service.description for the meta description. None of those description strings in data.ts mention a location. For queries like "vehicle branding Nairobi" or "DTF printing Kenya," an exact phrase match in the title tag is one of the strongest on-page signals — and it's the one thing missing from every single page.

Car & Fleet Branding | Brandmark Print Media
Car & Fleet Branding in Nairobi | Vehicle Wraps — Brandmark Print Media
FixAdd a metaTitle / metaDescription field per item in data.ts (or a small suffix rule in generateMetadata) that appends the city. See full table in Section 07.
High

Service & product H1s carry no location or intent modifier

Every service detail H1 is exactly the service name ("Branding & Signage", "Car & Fleet Branding") with nothing pairing it to Nairobi in the visible heading itself — even though the body copy below does mention Nairobi. Google weighs H1 text meaningfully; right now it's carrying pure category naming, not the actual phrase people type.

FixEither add a location line directly under the H1 (e.g. a subhead "Serving Nairobi & surrounding areas") or work "Nairobi" into the hero description sentence that immediately follows it on every service page — cheap, high-signal, no layout change needed.
Medium

Internal linking is structural, not contextual

Related-product and other-services links exist (good), but body copy rarely link-hops mid-sentence to a related product or service page — e.g. the "DTF Printing" service page never links out to the "Custom DTF T-Shirt" product, and vice versa, despite being an obvious, high-relevance pairing.

FixAdd 1–2 contextual in-copy links between tightly related service ↔ product pairs (DTF service ↔ DTF t-shirt; UV Printing service ↔ phone case/mug/bottle products; Laser Cutting service ↔ wall art/plaque products).
Low

No breadcrumb text differentiation for AI parsing

Visual breadcrumbs exist, but without BreadcrumbList schema they're only useful to human readers, not to search engines building a site hierarchy understanding.

FixCovered by the BreadcrumbList schema recommendation in Section 04 — same visual breadcrumb, just marked up.

04Structured data

Score 10/100

Zero JSON-LD anywhere on the site. This is the single most impactful gap to close relative to effort — the content needed for every recommended schema type already exists in data.ts; this is markup work, not content work.

Critical

No Organization / LocalBusiness schema on the homepage

This is the block that tells Google (and AI answer engines pulling from Google's Knowledge Graph) the business name, category, phone, address and hours in one machine-readable place — foundational for local pack eligibility and for AI Overviews to cite the business correctly by name.

FixAdd a LocalBusiness (subtype Store or ProfessionalService) JSON-LD block to the homepage: name, telephone: "+254719855331", address, areaServed: "Nairobi", sameAs (social profiles once live), priceRange.
High

No Product schema on any of the 12 shop items

Every product page already has a name, price (KSh), description and image — everything Product + Offer schema needs. Without it, products can't surface as rich results (price/availability snippets) and AI shopping-style answers have nothing structured to cite.

FixEmit Product JSON-LD per product page: name, image, description, offers.price (convert KSh to numeric), offers.priceCurrency: "KES", offers.availability. Since checkout is WhatsApp-based, set availability: "https://schema.org/InStock" and skip an Order-flow property.
High

No Service schema on any of the 8 service pages

Each service page already has name, description and an areaServed-equivalent (Nairobi, mentioned in body copy). This is a direct data-to-schema mapping with no new content required.

FixAdd Service JSON-LD: serviceType, provider (link to the Organization), areaServed: "Nairobi, Kenya", description.
High

No FAQPage schema despite every page having genuine, on-topic FAQs

Every service page ships 3 real FAQs, every product page ships 3 more, and the homepage has its own 6-item FAQ. FAQPage is exactly the schema type Google's rich-result FAQ snippets and AI Overview "People also ask"-style answers draw from most directly — and this content is 100% genuine (written for this business, not scraped or fabricated), so it's safe to mark up.

FixWrap the existing faqs arrays in data.ts into FAQPage JSON-LD on each service page, each product page, and the homepage FAQ section. This is the highest-ROI single schema addition on the site.
Medium

No BreadcrumbList schema

Visual breadcrumbs exist on every product and service page but carry no machine-readable hierarchy.

FixEmit BreadcrumbList JSON-LD matching the visible breadcrumb trail on product and service detail pages.

05Images

Score 65/100
next/image on every image — automatic resize, lazy-load, modern formats Alt text present on 100% of images — none missing
Medium

Service gallery images share one identical alt text

On each service detail page, all 2–4 "Recent work" gallery images use the exact same alt={"{"}service.name{"}"} — e.g. every image in the Branding & Signage gallery has alt text "Branding & Signage," with no differentiation between the four photos.

FixAdd a short per-image descriptor to the gallery arrays in data.ts (e.g. "Illuminated shopfront signage, dusk install" / "Reception acrylic lettering close-up") and interpolate into alt text.
Low

Alt text is functional but not keyword-descriptive

Product/service image alt text is just the item name (e.g. "Custom DTF T-Shirt"), which is correct and non-spammy, but doesn't work location or material context into image search — a missed low-effort channel for queries like "black DTF t-shirt Nairobi" in Google Images.

FixOptional enrichment: "Custom DTF-printed t-shirt — Brandmark Print Media, Nairobi" style alt text on primary product hero images only (not every thumbnail).
Low

Source image files are large (500–640KB)

Several AI-generated source JPEGs sit at 2048–2304px and 500–640KB before Next.js's on-the-fly optimization. Next/Image resizes and re-compresses at request time regardless, so delivered bytes to visitors are already much smaller — this only affects repo size and build time, not real-world page weight.

FixOptional: run a one-time sharp/squoosh pass to re-save originals at ~1600px/75% quality to shrink the repo, purely a housekeeping item.

06AI search readiness (GEO)

Score 40/100
Clean semantic HTML, real headings, list-formatted FAQs — easy for LLM crawlers to parse Direct, answer-shaped copy ("Most cut-out lettering starts from KSh 15,000...") — quotable as-is
What "GEO" actually means here: AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity mostly answer local-business questions by pulling from (a) structured data, (b) Google's Knowledge Graph / Business Profile, and (c) pages that state a direct, self-contained answer to a likely question. Brandmark's copy already does (c) reasonably well — the gap is (a) and (b), which are both zero right now.
High

No llms.txt

An emerging but low-cost convention: a plain-text file at the site root giving AI crawlers a direct summary of what the business does, its services, and canonical links — reduces the chance an LLM hallucinates or pulls stale info about the business.

FixAdd public/llms.txt: 1-paragraph business description, list of services with URLs, list of products with URLs, contact (phone/WhatsApp), location.
High

No structured data for AI to cite (see Section 04)

FAQPage and LocalBusiness schema aren't just a Google rich-result play — they're the two schema types most directly consumed by AI answer engines when constructing a cited local-business answer. This is the same fix as Section 04, counted here for its AI-specific payoff.

FixPrioritize FAQPage + LocalBusiness schema first; they carry the largest AI-citation return per hour of work.
Medium

No question-titled content matching how people ask AI assistants

ChatGPT/Perplexity-style queries skew conversational — "how much does a pull-up banner cost in Kenya," "is DTF or screen printing better for a small order." The site's pricing guides already answer several of these inline, but nothing is titled as the question itself, which is the format AI answer engines quote most directly.

FixSee Action Plan Phase 3 — light content hub with question-phrased titles reusing existing pricing/FAQ copy almost verbatim.
Low

No explicit brand-name + location pairing for disambiguation

"Brandmark" is a common naming pattern; without consistent "Brandmark Print Media, Nairobi" pairing in titles and schema, an AI system has a harder time disambiguating this business from similarly-named ones elsewhere.

FixNaturally resolved once titles/H1s/schema all carry the Nairobi qualifier per the fixes above — no separate action needed.

08Action plan

Phase 1 — Critical fixes

Week 1
  • Add src/app/sitemap.ts and src/app/robots.ts
  • Set metadataBase on the root layout once the domain is confirmed
  • Split /shop into a server-metadata wrapper + client grid; give /cart noindex
  • Rewrite title/description strings for all 20 dynamic pages to include "Nairobi" (table in Section 07)

Phase 2 — Structured data & AI readiness

Weeks 2–3
  • LocalBusiness JSON-LD on the homepage (name, phone, address, areaServed)
  • FAQPage JSON-LD on every service page, product page, and the homepage FAQ (content already written)
  • Product JSON-LD on all 12 shop items (price in KES, availability)
  • Service JSON-LD on all 8 service pages
  • BreadcrumbList JSON-LD matching existing visual breadcrumbs
  • Add public/llms.txt summarizing the business, services and products with URLs
  • Open Graph + Twitter Card metadata (reuse product/service hero images)

Phase 3 — Content & authority

Month 2
  • Add a real street address / area for NAP consistency and Google Business Profile setup
  • Weave contextual in-copy links between paired service/product pages (DTF, UV, laser-cut)
  • Unique alt text per service gallery image instead of one repeated string
  • 3–5 question-titled articles matching AI-search phrasing ("How much does vehicle branding cost in Nairobi?", "DTF vs screen printing — which is cheaper for a small order?")
  • Once real orders exist: replace generic testimonials with attributed, verifiable client reviews

Phase 4 — Monitoring & iteration

Ongoing, post-launch
  • Verify domain in Google Search Console; submit the new sitemap
  • Set up Google Business Profile with matching NAP once a public address is confirmed
  • Monitor real Core Web Vitals via CrUX/GSC — this audit's performance score is a lab estimate only, no live field data existed to check against
  • Re-run a full crawl-based audit after deployment to validate indexing, canonical resolution and live CWV
